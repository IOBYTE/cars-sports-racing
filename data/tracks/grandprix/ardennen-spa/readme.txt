ARDENNEN-SPA REWORKED

This track is a complete new work started from scratch to renovate the old Ardennen-Spa by @h-j-s (thank you very much for your track, I used it many years). It has been created using sd2-trackeditor , and can be entirely rebuilt and modified thanks to this utility and the AC3D objects provided in this zip.

Many thanks to @Madbad82 for the goal lights and the fences, and of course to @Torcs-ng (Xavier Bertaux) for his support and infinite patience, and @IOBYTE (Robert Reif) for creating the awesome tool that is Trackeditor,and for taking my suggestions into account.

Of course many thanks also to all the resources taken and modified from other tracks, such as textures or objects, thanks to all their creators.
