Cars Sports Racing 2.1.0
===============================
Fix Bugs
	* button challenge is not displayed on race 7
	* fix bug with level player and championship mode
	* In race mode, all races on current carset is allowed now

Cars
	* Added 91GP (Formula one 1991)
	* Added Supercars Class A
	* All cars have 3D instruments (activate or desactivate in advanced graphics options)

Simu
	* now setting camber & pressure for adjust tyre temperature

Racing
	* added contracts management in career mode
	* added speciale race in career mode (managed by fame level player)
	* added penalty on SEMI-PRO & PRO mode
	* penalty if a car collides with another in front (the penalty applied depends 
	  on the number of positions lost by the car in front), it will be checked by the race manager.
	* penalty if you too often hit the cars in front of you
	* added tuning cars in Career mode (if car is tuned by career these car is usuable in race mode)

Robots
	* added unit in strategy setup for axiom
	* Fix bug with strategy and compound for axiom

Graphics
	* Reintegrate OpenGL3 (OpenSceneGraph - WIP)
	* Added option shaders or not shader for Shadowmap (in OpenGL3)

Tracks:
	* Now possible section penalty cut curve (penalty drivethrough pitlane) otherwise this penalty is disabled.
